Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-11

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the app Habit (the “App”) processes data. The data controller is Zdunek at Poland, email: habit@zdunek.dev.

2. What data we process

The App stores data locally on your device using AsyncStorage, in particular:

  • habits, goals, and the selected active days;
  • completion log (e.g., check-ins per day);
  • App settings (e.g., light/dark theme, preferences).

The App does not create user accounts and does not collect directly identifying data (such as your name or email) unless you provide it voluntarily when contacting us.

3. Notifications and permissions

The reminders feature uses local notifications. The system will ask for your consent to send notifications. You can change your consent at any time in your device settings. The App does not send server push notifications and does not transmit device identifiers to the cloud.

4. Legal basis and purposes of processing

  • providing the service and ensuring the App works – GDPR Art. 6(1)(b);
  • your consent for notifications – GDPR Art. 6(1)(a);
  • legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention) – GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).

5. Data storage

Data is stored locally on your device for as long as you use the App. You can delete it by removing specific habits / logs inside the App, clearing the app data in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. Exceptions may apply where required by law (e.g., upon a lawful request from competent authorities).

7. External links

The App may contain links to external websites (e.g., the developer’s website or social profiles). After following an external link, their own privacy policy applies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it (“right to be forgotten”), restrict processing, data portability, and to object. To exercise your rights, contact us at habit@zdunek.dev.

9. Security

We apply appropriate organizational and technical measures adequate to the nature of the App, including data separation on the device and limiting permissions to the minimum necessary.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children. If you are under 16, use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. The new version will be published in the App with an effective date. For material changes we may display an additional notice in the App.
